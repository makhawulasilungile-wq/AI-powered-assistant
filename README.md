# AI CareerBoost Resume Assistant

## CAPACITI ASA 18 – AI-Powered Workplace Productivity Assistant

AI CareerBoost Resume Assistant is an AI-powered productivity solution designed to help job seekers create professional, ATS-friendly resumes and supporting career documents.

## Project Overview

Job seekers often spend significant time creating and rewriting resumes for different job applications. Many applicants also struggle to describe their experience professionally, identify relevant skills, and optimise their resumes for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

AI CareerBoost provides a guided workspace that helps users transform their verified education, experience, skills and career information into professional resume content.

## Target Users

- Graduates and students
- Unemployed job seekers
- Experienced professionals
- Career changers
- Applicants tailoring resumes for specific vacancies

## Core Features

### 1. Professional Summary Generator
Creates a professional, concise and ATS-friendly resume summary based only on information supplied by the user.

### 2. Work Experience Generator
Converts verified responsibilities into professional achievement-focused bullet points without inventing experience or statistics.

### 3. Skills Generator
Identifies relevant technical, professional, digital and transferable skills from the user's actual background and target job.

### 4. ATS Resume Optimizer
Compares the resume with a job description and identifies relevant keywords, missing areas and formatting improvements.

### 5. AI Career Chatbot
Provides career and resume assistance including summaries, skills, work experience, ATS guidance and cover letters.

### 6. Dynamic Resume Preview
Allows users to review their resume content in a structured resume layout.

### 7. Resume Templates
Provides professional resume presentation options.

### 8. Responsible AI Controls
The solution warns users against submitting inaccurate or fabricated information and encourages human verification.

## AI and Prompt Engineering

The project uses structured prompts containing:

- Role
- Context
- User information
- Task
- Constraints
- Output requirements
- Accuracy requirements
- Responsible AI instructions

The complete prompt library is available in the `prompts/` directory.

## Responsible AI

The assistant is designed to:

- Avoid fabricating qualifications or work experience
- Avoid inventing achievements or statistics
- Avoid recommending unsupported skills
- Reduce discriminatory or biased recommendations
- Encourage human verification
- Protect users from misleading AI-generated information
- Remind users that ATS optimisation does not guarantee employment

## Productivity Impact

The assistant reduces repetitive resume-writing work by allowing users to enter their verified career information once and use it to generate different resume sections.

It can help users:

1. Build a professional summary
2. Improve work experience descriptions
3. Identify relevant skills
4. Match their resume to a vacancy
5. Improve ATS readability
6. Generate a cover letter
7. Review their career documents

## Limitations
